How it works

From a box of parts to a robot that thinks, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Build

    Open the kit and put the robot together. Snap-fit parts and colour-coded connectors mean no soldering and no tools to lose.

  2. 02

    Connect

    The app walks them through wiring each sensor, motor and light, and explains what every connection does and why it matters.

  3. 03

    Code

    Bring it to life with drag-and-drop blocks. When they're ready, flip the switch and see the same program written in Python.

    FAQ

    Questions parents ask us.

    Does my child need any coding experience?

    None at all. Everyone starts with drag-and-drop blocks and guided projects. Python comes in gradually, once the ideas are familiar.

    Is soldering or special tooling required?

    No. Parts snap together and connectors are colour-coded, so kids can build safely at the kitchen table.

    What devices does the app run on?

    The CircuitCraft app is built for laptops and tablets. Kids connect to the robot over USB or wirelessly, depending on the kit.

    What age is CircuitCraft for?

    Kits are designed for ages 8–16. Younger builders do best with an adult nearby for the first project.

    Can I use it in a school or after-school club?

    Yes. Classroom packs include lesson plans and a teacher dashboard. Get in touch below and we'll help you plan.
