    FAQ

    Questions parents ask us.

    Does my child need any coding experience?

    None at all. Everyone starts with drag-and-drop blocks and guided projects, and Python comes in gradually once the ideas click.

    Is soldering or special tooling required?

    No. Parts snap together and connectors are colour-coded, so kids can build safely at the kitchen table.

    What devices does the app run on?

    The CircuitCraft app is built for laptops and tablets, connecting to the robot over USB or wirelessly.

    What age is CircuitCraft for?

    Ages 8–16. Younger builders do best with an adult nearby for their first project.
