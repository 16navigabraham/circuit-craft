What it does

Build

Snap the chassis, wheels and board together. No soldering, no lost screws.

Wire

The app shows where every wire goes and explains what each connection does.

Sense

Ultrasonic “eyes” measure distance so the robot can dodge what's in front of it.

Code

Drag-and-drop blocks to start. Flip a switch to see and write the same thing in Python.

Move

Press run and watch their code drive real motors on a real robot.

The parts

Five parts.
Each one teaches something.

The brain

Microcontroller

Where their code lives. It reads the sensors, makes decisions and tells the motors what to do.

Lesson: inputs, outputs and logic

The eyes

Ultrasonic sensor

Sends out a sound too high to hear and times the echo to work out how far away things are.

Lesson: sound, speed and distance

The heart

Battery pack

Rechargeable cells that power everything, and a safe way to learn about voltage and current.

Lesson: how electricity flows

The nerves

Circuit board & wires

Every connection is out in the open, so kids can trace exactly how signals travel.

Lesson: circuits and connections

The legs

Motors & wheels

Geared motors that turn code into movement, from straight lines to tight turns.

Lesson: motion, speed and direction

Questions

When can I get one?

We're building and testing the pilot robots now. Waitlist members hear first when kits are ready.

How much will it cost?

Pricing isn't final yet. Waitlist members will get early-bird pricing before anyone else.

Does my child need to know how to code?

No. Everyone starts with drag-and-drop blocks and guided projects, and Python comes in once the ideas click.

What age is it for?

Ages 8–16. Younger builders do best with an adult nearby for their first build.

Is soldering needed?

Never. Parts snap together and connectors are colour-coded, so it's safe at the kitchen table.

Can schools use it?

Yes. We're preparing classroom packs with lesson plans and a teacher dashboard. Choose “Educator” when you join the waitlist.
